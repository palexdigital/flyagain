About

Started in a hangar, not a boardroom

We're pilots from Wonderboom National Airport who spent our student years borrowing textbooks and breaking the bank on headsets. Every rand that went into gear was a rand that didn't go into the logbook.

FlyAgain started when we sold a spare Bose headset to a PPL student for half of retail and realised how many aviators were sitting on gear they no longer used — while the next generation of pilots couldn't afford to get started.

Today we buy, inspect, clean and resell pre-owned aviation equipment. Every headset is bench tested on both channels, every book checked against the current syllabus, every camera and tablet battery-health verified before it's listed.

Quality checked. Pilot approved. FlyAgain.

Our Mission

Flying is expensive. Gear shouldn't be.

At FlyAgain, our goal is to make aviation more affordable and accessible. We give pre-owned aviation appliances a second life so new and experienced aviators can focus on flying, not on breaking the bank.
